How this works

Most AI learning paths are designed for engineers who want to build models and AI systems from the ground up. My goal is different. I am building the technical depth required to become an AI Technical Program Manager — enough to understand engineering decisions, architecture, AI systems, risks, trade-offs and delivery without becoming a full-time ML engineer.

The curriculum is organized into 12 levels and 61 focused lessons, progressing from software and AI foundations through LLMs, RAG, agents, MCP, production AI, AI product management and AI TPM. Each lesson combines technical concepts with a TPM perspective, practical exercises and knowledge checks.
